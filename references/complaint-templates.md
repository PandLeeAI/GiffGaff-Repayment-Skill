# 申诉英文模板（模块化 · 按诉求拼装）

> 所有模板保留英文原貌（giffgaff / Communications Ombudsman 只认英文）。`[占位符]` 替换为用户真实信息后复制使用。
> 模板为**模块化**：先从下方"各诉求主张段"中选出用户勾选的诉求，拼进"通用开头/结尾"即可生成范文。没选的诉求不强行塞入。

---

## 一、通用开头（Formal Complaint 用）

**Subject：** `Formal Complaint - Request to restore service, preserve number and refund unused balance`
（标题按用户实际诉求组合调整，例如只追退款可改为 `Formal Complaint - Refund of unused credit`）

```
Dear giffgaff Complaints Team,

I am making a formal complaint regarding the disconnection of my mobile service.

I received a notification stating that my service was disconnected because my usage pattern suggested long-term use outside the UK.

I understand that giffgaff services are designed primarily for UK use. However, I believe this decision does not accurately reflect my circumstances and I would like my case to be reviewed.
```

## 二、各诉求主张段（按需选取拼装）

**【保号 / 恢复账号+号码】**
```
I request that my account and mobile service are restored if possible. This number is linked to important online accounts and services, and immediate loss of the number creates significant access and security difficulties. If restoration is not possible, I request that giffgaff preserve my mobile number and prevent it from being recycled, and provide me with a workable method to obtain a PAC so that I can preserve my number.
```

**【退款 / 未使用余额原路退回】**
```
If giffgaff cannot continue to provide service, I request a refund of my remaining unused credit and any applicable unused balance to my original payment method. My account still contained [£XX.XX] of unused credit when the service was disconnected.
```

**【赔偿 / 话费补偿 / Goodwill Credit】**
```
I would also like giffgaff to provide appropriate compensation or goodwill credit for the inconvenience, loss of service, disruption to access to my linked accounts, and the time and effort required to resolve this matter.
```

**【携号转网 / PAC】**
```
If the service cannot be restored, I request that giffgaff preserve my mobile number and provide a workable method to obtain a PAC without requiring SMS verification to the already-disconnected SIM, so that I can port my number to another carrier.
```

> 提示（原文 §19）：PAC 解决的是号码归属/携号转网，退款解决的是未使用预付余额，两者不是同一问题，**可以同时主张**。若用户选了"退款+转网"，把两段都拼进去即可，无需二选一。

## 三、通用结尾（Formal Complaint 用）

```
Please treat this as a Formal Complaint and provide me with a complaint reference number.

Username: [你的giffgaff用户名]
Mobile number: [你的英国手机号]

Kind regards,
[你的姓名]
```

## 三·B、完整参考：可直接发送的 Formal Complaint（原文 §5 范本）

> 上面是模块化拼装；若想直接用一封信，可复制下方原文范本（已内嵌"恢复账号 → 若不行则保留号码+人工 PAC → 退还未使用余额"的优先级，避免被理解为接受终止服务）。按需替换 `[占位符]`。

**Subject：** `Formal Complaint - Request to restore service, preserve number and refund unused balance`

```
Dear giffgaff Complaints Team,

I am making a formal complaint regarding the disconnection of my mobile service.

I received a notification stating that my service was disconnected because my usage pattern suggested long-term use outside the UK.

I understand that giffgaff services are designed primarily for UK use. However, I believe this decision does not accurately reflect my circumstances and I would like my case to be reviewed.

I request that my account and mobile service are restored if possible.

If restoration is not possible, I request that giffgaff:
1. Preserve my mobile number and prevent it from being recycled;
2. Provide me with a workable method to obtain a PAC so that I can preserve my number;
3. Refund my remaining unused credit and any applicable unused balance to my original payment method.

This number is linked to important online accounts and services, and immediate loss of the number creates significant access and security difficulties.

Please treat this as a Formal Complaint and provide me with a complaint reference number.

Username: [你的giffgaff用户名]
Mobile number: [你的英国手机号]

Kind regards,
[你的姓名]
```

## 四、PAC 补充申诉（发现"停号后 PAC 要短信验证"的坑后，回复原 Ticket，不要开新投诉）

> 若用户同时追退款，在正文 `My priority is preserving my mobile number.` 之后追加一句：`If the service cannot be restored, I also request a refund of my remaining unused credit to my original payment method.`

```
Dear giffgaff Complaints Team,

I would like to add an important issue regarding preservation of my mobile number.

I understand that requesting a PAC is the recommended way to preserve my number. However, the PAC request process requires SMS verification to my giffgaff mobile number. As my mobile service has already been disconnected by giffgaff, I cannot receive the verification SMS and therefore cannot complete the PAC request.

Please therefore either:
1. Restore my service; or
2. Provide an alternative verification method; or
3. Manually assist me in obtaining my PAC.

My priority is preserving my mobile number.

Kind regards,
[姓名]
```

## 五、Ombudsman 争议描述（"What is your dispute about?" 填写框）

```
I am disputing giffgaff's decision to permanently disconnect my Pay As You Go mobile service and close my account under its Fair Usage Policy.

My mobile number is [YOUR NUMBER]. I topped up my account and made very limited actual use of the service. My account still contained unused credit when the service was disconnected.

giffgaff states that my device had been connected to international networks for an extended period and therefore concluded that my usage was inconsistent with temporary roaming.

I submitted a Formal Complaint and asked giffgaff to review the decision and restore my account and mobile service. However, giffgaff refused to reinstate my account and subsequently issued its Final Response.

I believe the permanent disconnection and closure of my account should be independently reviewed for fairness and proportionality, particularly given the limited actual paid usage and the importance of the mobile number to me.

The disconnection has also created a serious practical problem. The number is linked to important online accounts and services. giffgaff's Final Response advised me that I could preserve my number by requesting a PAC. However, the PAC process requires SMS verification to the same giffgaff number that has already been disconnected. I therefore cannot receive the verification SMS and cannot complete the PAC process offered by giffgaff.

I am asking the Communications Ombudsman to independently review the decision to permanently disconnect my service and the way giffgaff has handled the consequences of that decision.
```

## 六、Ombudsman 沟通说明（"How have you contacted the supplier?" 填写框）

```
I submitted a Formal Complaint through giffgaff's official website complaint channel. The complaint was registered with a Complaint Ticket and reference number. Communication regarding the complaint was handled through giffgaff's online Complaint/Messenger system, with notifications and responses also sent to my registered email address. I asked giffgaff to review the disconnection and restore my account and mobile number. giffgaff subsequently issued its Final Response through the complaint process. It refused to restore my account or service. The Final Response advised me to request a PAC to preserve my number. However, the PAC process requires SMS verification to the same number that giffgaff has already disconnected, leaving me unable to complete the process normally.
```

## 七、Ombudsman 诉求声明（"What would you like the supplier to do?" 填写框，按用户诉求拼装）

> 拼装规则：下方每段是独立主张，只保留用户勾选诉求对应的段，未选的不塞入。选了"保号"则以保号段为主诉求开头；没选保号，直接从所选诉求段组织，把"备选"提升为主诉求。

**【保号 / 恢复账号+号码】**（用户选了"保号"时保留）
```
My primary requested resolution is for giffgaff to restore my account and reinstate my existing mobile number with working mobile service.

Keeping this number is extremely important to me because it is linked to multiple important online accounts and services. I therefore ask that the number is protected from recycling while this dispute is being considered.
```

**【赔偿 / 话费补偿 / Goodwill Credit】**（用户选了"赔偿"时保留）
```
I would also like giffgaff to provide appropriate compensation or goodwill credit for the inconvenience, loss of service, disruption to access to my linked accounts, and the time and effort required to resolve this matter.
```

**【备选：转网 / PAC】**（用户选了"转网"、且保号不可行时保留）
```
If restoration is not possible, I request that my mobile number is preserved and giffgaff provides a workable method to obtain a PAC without requiring SMS verification to the already disconnected SIM, so that I can port my number to another carrier.
```

**【备选：退款】**（用户选了"退款"、且保号不可行时保留）
```
If restoration is not possible, I request that my remaining unused credit is refunded to my original payment method.
```

**【组合示例】**
- 仅退款：以"备选：退款"段作为诉求主体，开头可写 `My requested resolution is a refund of my remaining unused credit to my original payment method.`
- 保号 + 退款：保号段 + "备选：退款"段（即"若无法恢复则退款"，对应原文 §19"PAC 与退款可同时主张"）。
- 全选：保号段 + 赔偿段 + （若论证恢复不可行）转网段 + 退款段。
```
## 八、退款到账后继续追号码（仅退款已到、号码未恢复时使用）

```
Thank you for processing the refund. I confirm that the refund issue has now been resolved. However, my primary complaint remains unresolved. My preferred resolution has always been the restoration of my giffgaff account and mobile number. I therefore do not consider the entire dispute resolved solely because the unused credit has been refunded. Please continue to consider my request for restoration of my account and existing mobile number, together with appropriate compensation for the disruption caused.
```
